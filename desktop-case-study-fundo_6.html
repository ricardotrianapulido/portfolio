Fintech Conversion Optimization

Redesigning a fintech origination journey through continuous experimentation, AI-assisted execution, and user data to improve conversion, satisfaction, and delivery quality.

Conversion +35% Across key funnel outcomes
Quality ~0 UI-related bugs in the new flow
Satisfaction +10 NPS improvement after the redesign
Executive Summary

A data-driven redesign of a fintech origination journey focused on improving conversion, trust, and delivery quality.

Context

A fintech company needed to improve a complex origination flow where users had to complete several steps before receiving a funding decision.

Challenge

The flow had friction across key steps, causing drop-offs, uncertainty, and operational inconsistency.

My Role

I led UX strategy, experimentation, visual redesign, front-end execution, qualitative and quantitative analysis, and stakeholder storytelling.

Outcome

The work improved step progression, completed applications, funded conversion, NPS, delivery speed, and UI quality.

The Challenge

A high-intent funnel with too much friction.

The origination flow was business-critical, but users were dropping off before completing their application or reaching funding outcomes. Three main challenges were:

01. Funnel friction
Up to 75% drop-off across early and mid-funnel steps.
02. Trust and clarity gaps
New customers lacked the context and reassurance needed to trust the process.
03. Operational dependency
Internal tools and front-end execution needed to become faster, cleaner, and more reliable.

05 Evidence & Key Insights
01
Funnel friction compounds fast

Small moments of uncertainty created major drop-offs across early and mid-funnel steps.

02
Trust drives conversion in fintech

New customers needed more context, reassurance, and clarity before sharing sensitive information.

03
Execution speed affects learning speed

UI inconsistencies and front-end dependency slowed down the team's ability to test and improve the funnel.
