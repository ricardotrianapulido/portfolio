Trust-Centered Dating App Onboarding

Redesigning onboarding and premium upgrade moments to build trust, improve motivation, and make value easier to understand.

Completion 91% Registration completion

01 Executive Summary

A product strategy and onboarding redesign focused on trust, motivation, and perceived value.

Context

A dating app needed to improve an onboarding experience that felt long, unclear, and disconnected from the product's emotional value.

Challenge

Users were being asked to share personal information before understanding why it mattered or how it would improve their experience.

My Role

I led UX strategy, research, prioritization, stakeholder alignment, prototyping, validation, and AI-supported workflow acceleration.

Outcome

The redesign improved registration completion, total conversion, usability perception, and the clarity of the premium upgrade experience.

The Challenge

A high-emotion product with low trust and unclear motivation.

The onboarding experience asked users for personal information before making the value of the product clear. This created friction, hesitation, and weak motivation to complete the flow.

01. Long and unclear onboarding
Users were asked to complete too many steps before understanding the value of the experience.
02. Sensitive data without context
The flow requested personal information without clearly explaining why it mattered.
03. Weak premium value proposition
The upgrade moment lacked clarity, timing, and a strong reason to convert.
